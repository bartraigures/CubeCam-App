Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

CubeCam is a camera app for iPhone. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In brief

CubeCam has no accounts and no servers, and everything it does happens on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from the app to its developer: no photographs, analytics, crash reports, usage data, identifiers or location.

The camera

CubeCam uses the camera to show the viewfinder and to take photographs. Viewfinder images are processed on the iPhone's graphics chip and are neither stored nor sent anywhere. Only the photographs you take are kept.

Your photo library

CubeCam saves the photographs you take, and Apple's original if you choose to keep it, to the photo library on your iPhone. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs them in the same way as any other photograph.

The photo viewer asks for permission to read your library so that it can show the photographs CubeCam has taken, change their look and show how they were taken. It only shows CubeCam's own photographs and the ones you choose to add to it, and limited access is enough. Changes made in the viewer and the colour editor are saved as revertible edits to the photograph in your library, and iOS asks for your permission each time.

When you share photographs to CubeCam from another app, the share sheet hands CubeCam a copy, which waits in CubeCam's storage on the iPhone until you next open the app. CubeCam then finds the same photograph in your library, or saves it there if it isn't already, and deletes its copy.

What is kept on the iPhone

CubeCam keeps your settings, such as the look, the layout, the grain and the lens and saving choices. It keeps a list of the photographs it has saved, with the look and settings each was taken with, so that the viewer can show and re-grade them. It also holds each photograph briefly, between taking it and saving it to the library, so that no shot is lost, and likewise any photograph shared to it until the app next opens. Looks you import or make in the colour editor are kept too. All of this stays in CubeCam's own storage on the iPhone and is removed if the app is deleted. Photographs already in your library stay there.

The locked camera

When CubeCam is opened on a locked iPhone, the photographs taken are kept in a private folder provided by iOS and moved into your library the next time CubeCam is opened with the phone unlocked. A copy of your settings is passed to the locked camera through iOS, on the iPhone itself.

What CubeCam doesn't do

CubeCam doesn't collect, sell or share personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking code or third-party software development kits, and it doesn't use your location.

Children

CubeCam collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If CubeCam changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
